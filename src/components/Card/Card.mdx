# Card

Cards group related information and actions into a single, structured container.

## Usage Guidelines
* Use cards to present bite-sized info (such as patient stats, lab summaries, or dashboard metrics).
* Keep content inside a card focused on a single topic or entity.
* Interactive cards should have a clear hover and focus state across the entire clickable surface.

## Do's and Don'ts

| Do 🟢 | Don't 🔴 |
| :--- | :--- |
| Group related sub-components (Header, Body, Footer) consistently. | Overcrowd cards with complex forms or nested tables. |
| Maintain consistent padding and shadow elevation across cards in a grid. | Mix different card hierarchy styles arbitrarily in the same layout. |

## Accessibility Notes
* **Structure:** Use semantic headings (`<h3>`, `<h4>`) inside card titles to maintain proper document outline hierarchy.
* **Clickable Surface:** If the whole card is interactive, wrap the card or title cleanly to avoid duplicate screen reader navigation paths.
